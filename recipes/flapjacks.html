    Ingredients

  • Raisins
  • Maple syrup
  • Porridge oats
  • Butter

    Steps

  1. Melt the butter
  2. Mix the melted butter with the porridge oats
  3. Add raisins to the mix if using
  4. Finally pour in maple syrup and mix
  5. Shape mix into a flat tray bake tin
  6. Bake on a low temperature until firm
  7. While warm cut into desired shape
  8. Allow to cool and enjoy
