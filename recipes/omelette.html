Omelette

Home

    Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Butter
  • Cheese
  • Salt
  • Pepper

    Steps

  1. Add butter to hot pan and melt
  2. Whisk eggs in a bowl
  3. Season egg mixture with salt and pepper
  4. Add seasoned egg mix to the hot pan with butter and cook
  5. Grate cheese on top of omelette mix
  6. If using top omelette with ham
  7. Serve
